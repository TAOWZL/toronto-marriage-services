Common questions

Straight answers to what couples ask most.

Is this a real, legally valid marriage?

Yes. It is a full legal marriage under Ontario's Marriage Act, performed by a registered officiant and filed with the Office of the Registrar General. The only difference from a big wedding is that we skip the party — the legal part is identical and fully valid, including for immigration.

How fast can we get married?

Once you have your marriage licence (available same-day at most city halls), we can often book you within days. The signing itself takes about 20 minutes.

Do we need witnesses?

Yes — Ontario law requires two witnesses (16 or older) present at the signing, in addition to the officiant. Most couples bring a friend or family member. If you can't bring two, let us know when booking and we can provide one for a small fee.

Do you travel to us?

No. All signings take place at our Riverdale office at 255 Broadview Avenue, a short walk from Broadview Station. This keeps appointments fast, private, and on time.

What about our Marriage Certificate?

After we file your licence, the Province processes it and you apply for your official Marriage Certificate (typically a few weeks later). We give you the exact steps, and this certificate is what immigration and other authorities accept.

Do we need to exchange vows or rings?

No. Ontario law requires only two short spoken declarations, which we guide you through. Vows and rings are optional — this is the simple legal version.
