Questions

Good to know

Is this a real, legally valid marriage?

Yes. It is a full legal marriage under Ontario's Marriage Act, performed by a registered officiant and filed with the Office of the Registrar General. The only thing we skip is the celebration — the legal part is identical and fully valid, including for immigration.

How fast can we get married?

Once you have your marriage licence (available same day at most city halls), we can often book you within days. The signing itself takes about 20 minutes.

What about witnesses?

Ontario law requires two witnesses aged 16 or older, present at the signing, in addition to the officiant. Bring your own two, or if you're short one, we can provide a single witness for a small fee — you'll need at least one of your own.

When are appointments available?

Weekday mornings or evenings, by appointment. Exact times vary by date — call or text and we'll find a slot that works.

Do you travel to us?

No. All signings take place at our Riverdale office at 255 Broadview Avenue, a short walk from Broadview Station. This keeps every appointment private and on time.

What about our Marriage Certificate?

After we file your licence, the Province processes it and you apply for your official Marriage Certificate (typically a few weeks later). We give you the exact steps — this certificate is what immigration and other authorities accept.

Do we need vows or rings?

No. Ontario law requires only two short spoken declarations, which we guide you through. Vows and rings are optional — this is the simple legal version.
