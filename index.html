Questions

Good to know

Is this a real, legally valid marriage?

Yes. It is a full legal marriage under Ontario's Marriage Act, performed by a registered officiant and filed with the Registrar General. The only thing we skip is the party. The legal marriage is exactly the same, and it gives you the official record that government offices and other authorities ask for.

How fast can we get married?

Once you have your marriage licence, which most city halls issue the same day, we can often book you within a few days. The signing itself takes about 30 minutes.

What about witnesses?

Ontario asks for two witnesses at the signing, and the officiant cannot be one of them. Bring your own two, or if you are short one, we can provide a witness for a small fee. You will need at least one of your own.

When are appointments available?

Weekday mornings or evenings, by appointment. The exact times change with the date, so call or text and we will find a slot that works for you.

Do you travel to us?

No. Every signing happens at our Riverdale office at 255 Broadview Avenue. That keeps your appointment private and on time.

What about our Marriage Certificate?

On the day, you get a Record of Solemnization to keep. It is a memento, not the official certificate. After we file, the Province registers your marriage and you apply for the official Marriage Certificate, which can take up to about eight weeks after registration. That certificate is the one government offices accept, and we give you the exact steps to order it.

Do we need vows or rings?

No. The legal part is a short spoken exchange that we guide you through. No vows, rings, readings, or guests are needed. This is the simple legal version.
